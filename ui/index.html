Play it from the keyboard

Left hand runs deck 1, right hand deck 2. Hold CUE or a hot cue to preview; hold slower/faster to nudge. Shift with: a hot cue deletes it, slower/faster steps the tempo 0.1%, a jump goes 16 beats, a stem solos it.

↑↓ move in the list · Enter load onto a free deck · Shift+←/→ onto deck 1 / 2
Drag a song onto a deck, or onto Auto-DJ · drag the big waveform to scratch · click the small one to jump
Stems: click VOX / MEL / BASS / DRUM, right-click to solo · right-click a hot cue to delete it · double-click a knob to reset it

On the controller

Browse knob scrolls the list (SHIFT: zoom), push switches playlists/songs · LOAD loads, LOAD twice = instant double of the other deck · SHIFT + LOAD sorts by BPM (deck 1) or key (deck 2)
4 BEAT/EXIT: a 4-beat loop or out of it · CUE/LOOP CALL ◀▶: halve/double a loop, else step through memory cues (none saved: jump 4 beats) · SHIFT + ▶ saves a memory cue, SHIFT + ◀ deletes one
BEAT SYNC: sync on/off (hold: on) · SHIFT + BEAT SYNC: tempo range · SHIFT + a channel fader or the crossfader: fader start (up plays, back down returns to the cue)
SMART FADER: the faders blend tempo and bass too (SHIFT: Auto-DJ on/off) · SMART CFX: FILTER knobs add an effect (SHIFT: next effect)
Pads. HOT CUE: 8 cues (SHIFT deletes) · PAD FX 1/2: hold for roll, echo, trans… · BEAT JUMP: ∓1 2 4 8 beats (SHIFT ×16) · SAMPLER: the eight sounds (SHIFT stops one)
SHIFT + mode. KEYBOARD = stems: 1-4 on/off (SHIFT solos), 5 acapella, 6 instrumental, 7 no drums, 8 all · BEAT LOOP: ¼ to 32 beats (SHIFT: loop roll while held) · KEY SHIFT: top row −2 −1 +1 +2 semitones (SHIFT −12 −7 +7 +12), bottom row match the other deck, down, up, reset
SHIFT + headphone CUE: key lock · MASTER CUE: master in phones (SHIFT: quantize) · SHIFT + BEAT ◀: effect back to 1 beat · SHIFT + BEAT ▶: effect on the master
Your own sampler sounds: put 1.wav … 16.wav in the samples folder beside Slipmat's settings and restart (pads 9-16 are deck 2's)
