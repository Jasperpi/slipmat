Shortcuts

↑↓ move in the list
Shift+← load onto deck 1 · Shift+→ deck 2
/ search Spotify
Z cue · X play deck 1
, cue · . play deck 2
1–4 hot cues deck 1 · 7–0 deck 2
Drag a song onto a deck to load it
Drag the big waveform to scratch; click the small one to jump
Right-click a hot cue to delete it
Stems: click VOX / MEL / BASS / DRUM to drop one out, right-click to solo it
Double-click a knob or fader to reset it

On the controller

Browse knob scrolls the list, LOAD loads the highlighted song
PAD FX modes: hold a pad for roll, echo, trans and more
4 BEAT/EXIT: a 4-beat loop, or out of the loop (SHIFT: back into it) · CUE/LOOP CALL ◀▶ halve/double
SHIFT + browse knob zooms the waveforms · MASTER CUE puts the master in your headphones
SHIFT + BEAT SYNC changes the tempo range
SHIFT + headphone CUE toggles key lock
SHIFT + HOT CUE (keyboard mode) = stems: pads 1-4 vocals, melody, bass, drums on/off (SHIFT solos); 5 acapella, 6 instrumental, 7 no drums, 8 all back
